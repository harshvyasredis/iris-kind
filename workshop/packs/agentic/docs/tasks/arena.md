# Enter the Gateway Agent Arena

Choose **Run my agent**.

Your score combines five cases:

- three retrieval/routing decisions;
- one delayed soft opt-out;
- one cross-brand cache isolation check.

## Win conditions

1. **Accuracy:** pass all five cases.
2. **Prompt tokens:** lower than the naive reference.
3. **Peak context:** no dump-all retrieval.
4. **p50 latency:** avoid unnecessary sequential calls.

Accuracy is the gate. A fast, cheap wrong answer does not win.

The gold cases are in the workspace because this is a workshop, not an
anti-cheat system. Improve the three agent modules rather than editing the
arena or expected results.

When several teams finish, compare the four numbers—not just the pass count.
Ask the leading team what it excluded from context. Exclusion is often the
most important retrieval decision.
